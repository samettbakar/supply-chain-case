# supply-chain-case

Anonymized womenswear planning case on GitHub.

## Problem

A womenswear planner needs to align price, assortment, production capacity and sales channels with uncertain demand. The case examines how those choices affect stock risk and cash.

## Approach

- Reviewed the assortment, pricing tiers, sizes and sales channels in the Brand X case.
- Identified the operating assumptions and data needed to test stock and capacity decisions.
- Used the separate TGIF concept to examine minimum order quantities, lead times and inventory risk.

## Case documents

- [Brand X womenswear planning case](brand-x-dtc-womenswear.md)
- [TGIF operations concept](tgif-operations.md)

## Status

Public

## Data

All data is anonymized. The Brand X case uses public observations and sector assumptions. TGIF is a self-initiated concept with illustrative figures.

## Limitations

The documents describe planning scenarios and assumptions. Their figures should be read as examples until validated with operating data.
